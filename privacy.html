Last Updated: November 27, 2026

FarroouuX Studio ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you play our mobile game, Sweet Blast Deluxe.

1. Information We Collect

Our game utilizes Google Play Games Services and Firebase. We may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Your Google Play Games ID and public username to enable cloud saves and display leaderboards.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use this data strictly to provide core game features such as cloud saves, live tournaments, and friend challenges. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

Our game integrates with Google Play Services and Google AdMob. These services may collect data to provide personalized ads and analytics in accordance with their own respective privacy policies.

4. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You have the right to access or delete your game data at any time. You can do this by clearing the app data in your device settings, or by contacting our support team directly.
